Every COD order that comes back costs you twice.

Pakka scores each cash-on-delivery order for return-to-origin (RTO) risk the moment it's placed, then confirms it on WhatsApp or nudges the shopper to prepay before you ship.

Score an orderEstimate my savings
  • 1. Score each COD order at checkout using order, address and history signals.
  • 2. Act automatically: ship, confirm on WhatsApp, offer a prepaid discount, or hold.
  • 3. Learn from delivery outcomes so the model sharpens for your store.
